# LikBy AI & Machine Learning Strategy

---

## 1. Philosophical Grounding: Guardrails First

At LikBy, AI is treated as an optional enhancement, never as a single point of failure or an autonomous decision-maker over sensitive user rights:
- **Deterministic Core:** Match eligibility, quota enforcement, subscription activation, and payment reconciliation are 100% deterministic code. No LLM or black-box ML model can grant entitlements, bypass blocks, or alter financial ledgers.
- **Graceful Fallback:** If any AI service is offline, slow, or fails, the core platform remains completely functional with deterministic fallbacks.

---

## 2. High-Value AI Use Cases

1. **Contextual Icebreakers (Optional):** Suggesting friendly, non-intrusive conversation starters upon establishing a new mutual match based on shared declared languages and bio keywords.
2. **Abuse & Toxicity Pre-Filtering:** Assisting human moderators by scoring reported text messages for severe harassment, hate speech, or spam patterns.
3. **Smart Bio Suggestions:** Optional, user-invoked suggestions to help users polish their public bio during onboarding.
