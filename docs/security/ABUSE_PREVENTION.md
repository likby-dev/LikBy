# LikBy Abuse Prevention & Community Trust

---

## 1. Moderation Architecture

1. **User Blocking:** Instant, reciprocal, and permanent until unblocked. Blocks chat relay and discovery.
2. **Actionable Reporting (`/report`):** Users can report spam, harassment, inappropriate media, or impersonation.
3. **Karma & Rating Engine:** Post-session ratings track bad actors; repeated low ratings trigger automated cooldown periods.
4. **Age Verification:** Age 18+ enforcement on profile creation prevents minor participation.
