# LikBy Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Product Vision:** LikBy (*Meet. Connect. Play.*) is a premium, privacy-centric social discovery and gaming platform designed for meaningful human connections without intrusive tracking, spam, or ephemeral gimmicks.

---

## 1. Core Value Propositions

1. **Safety & Privacy:** Complete user control over personal data, zero advertisements, and default content protection (`protect_content=True`) on all shared media.
2. **Non-Negotiable Persistence:** No artificial disappearing chats, vanishing photos, or self-destruct timers. Conversations and memories persist until deliberate, user-initiated deletion.
3. **Engaging Casual Gaming:** 17 integrated games (11 competitive duels and 6 relaxing solo games) playable directly in Telegram and Mini Apps.
4. **Transparent & Respectful Tiers:** A generous Base tier with predictable daily limits, complemented by Spark, Premium, and Diamond upgrades that empower power users without punishing free users.

---

## 2. User Personas

- **The Casual Socializer:** Seeks genuine, safe conversations and casual games with like-minded individuals. Prefers Telegram-native simplicity and respects content protection.
- **The Competitive Gamer:** Loves challenging friends to turn-based and arcade duels (Carrom Clash, Connect Four, Air Rush) and climbing social leaderboards.
- **The Privacy-Conscious Explorer:** Strongly values zero-advertising, the absence of public phone number disclosures, and durable, tamper-resistant data governance.
