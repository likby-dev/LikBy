# LikBy Notification Policy & Anti-Spam Controls

---

## 1. Notification Categories & Privacy

1. **Matches:** High priority. Dispatched immediately when a mutual like is established.
2. **Direct Messages & Media:** High priority. Relayed in real-time with content protection.
3. **Game Invitations:** Standard priority. Includes actionable "Accept" and "Decline" inline buttons.
4. **Subscription Reminders:** Sent 24 hours prior to expiration and immediately upon renewal.

---

## 2. Respectful Communication Rules

- **Zero Marketing Broadcasts:** No unsolicited promotional messages or advertisements are ever sent.
- **Quiet Hours:** Users can configure local quiet hours in `/settings`.
- **Message Batching:** Rapid successive interactions from the same user are debounced to prevent notification bombardment.
