# LikBy 17-Game Master Catalog

All 17 games are registered in `src/likby/games/registry.py` with server-authoritative rule engines.

---

## 1. 11 Duel Games (Turn-Based & Competitive)

1. **`connect_four` (Connect Four):** Classic vertical grid strategy (7 columns x 6 rows). Connect 4 discs to win.
2. **`carrom_clash` (Carrom Clash):** Turn-based tabletop striker game. Pocket carrom men and the queen.
3. **`air_rush` (Air Rush):** Fast-paced air hockey duel. First to 5 goals or highest score at timeout wins.
4. **`pocket_pool` (Pocket Pool):** Turn-based 8-ball billiards simulation with angle calculation.
5. **`stick_arena` (Stick Arena):** Turn-based tactical battle arena with attack, block, and special moves.
6. **`quickdraw` (Quickdraw):** Drawing and guessing duel with sanitized keyword verification.
7. **`word_rush` (Word Rush):** Competitive anagram and vocabulary battle with dictionary checks.
8. **`brain_blitz` (Brain Blitz):** Head-to-head general knowledge and trivia showdown.
9. **`reflex_rush` (Reflex Rush):** Millisecond reaction challenge with strict false-start penalties.
10. **`mindmatch` (MindMatch):** Social telepathy game where players try to submit matching answers.
11. **`mystery_mission` (Mystery Mission):** Cooperative 2-player deduction puzzle.

---

## 2. 6 Solo Games (Uncapped Plays for Base Tier)

1. **`zen_merge` (Zen Merge):** Relaxing 2048-style block and number merging.
2. **`word_hunt` (Word Hunt):** Grid-based word search with progressive difficulty.
3. **`color_flow` (Color Flow):** Connecting matching color nodes without overlapping paths.
4. **`pocket_golf` (Pocket Golf):** Casual physics-based mini golf puzzle.
5. **`reflex_lab` (Reflex Lab):** Reaction time and cognitive training drills.
6. **`daily_mystery` (Daily Mystery):** Daily logic and riddle challenge with fresh puzzles every 00:00 UTC.
